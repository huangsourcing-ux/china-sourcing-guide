# Mattress Flammability Checks Before Shipping from China

> Published: August 13, 2026
> Updated: September 27, 2026
> Original: https://www.huangsourcing.com/mattress-flammability-checks-china

Check mattress prototypes, 16 CFR 1632 and 1633 evidence, materials, labels, production lots, compressed packs, and release decisions before China shipment.

Before releasing balance payment or pickup for mattresses made in China, connect each finished model and production lot to its qualified prototype, flammability records, current material build, permanent label, General Certificate of Conformity data, compressed package, and carton map. September 2026 U.S. recalls of Kesyup and WinxMatrs mattresses make the model-to-lot evidence question immediate. Hold the shipment when the records and physical goods do not describe the same controlled version.

## Quick Answer

Use three separate gates: importer-led scope and rule review, qualified 16 CFR parts 1632 and 1633 evidence for the relevant prototype, and buyer-side comparison of sampled production with approved references. Before release, list each SKU, prototype ID, manufacture date, production lot, compressed pack, and carton range in one reconciliation. A routine inspection can document visible identity and traceability; it cannot reproduce prescribed flammability tests or certify compliance.

## September 2026 public case

On September 24, 2026, the U.S. CPSC announced a recall of about 48,405 China-made Kesyup mattresses. Its [official notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/ZCK01-Recalls-Kesyup-Mattresses-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Fire-Hazard-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Mattress-Flammability) says they violate the mandatory mattress flammability standard. It identifies four sizes, compressed packaging, and a sewn-in prototype ID label. No incidents or injuries were reported. The notice does not provide the full test file or a root-cause finding.

A separate [WinxMatrs recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Xinan-Home-Recalls-WinxMatrs-Mattresses-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Fire-Hazard-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Mattress-Flammability) the same day covered about 792 China-made compressed twin mattresses. The same-day [Emerspring notice](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Style-Homeware-Recalls-Emerspring-Mattresses-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Fire-Hazard-Violate-Mandatory-Standard-for-Mattress-Flammability) covered about 263 mattresses made in Indonesia, with a different remedy. These are distinct cases and do not establish a shared supplier or failure cause.

Before releasing payment or pickup, reconcile the SKU, tested prototype, approved material build, sewn label, production date, lot, compressed pack, and carton range. The importer and qualified specialists decide whether the records support the product; a visual inspection cannot prove flammability compliance.
