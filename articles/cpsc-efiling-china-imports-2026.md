# CPSC eFiling for China Imports: Post-Launch Buyer Checks

> Published: June 22, 2026
> Updated: August 16, 2026
> Original: https://www.huangsourcing.com/cpsc-efiling-china-imports-2026

CPSC eFiling is live. Check China-side product IDs, testing, certificate data, exclusions, and broker handoff before payment or shipment release.

CPSC eFiling is now in effect for most covered U.S.-bound regulated consumer products. CPSC announced full implementation on July 8, 2026; the later January 8, 2027 date applies to covered products entered from a Foreign Trade Zone. Buyers sourcing from China should organize the product-to-certificate evidence and broker handoff before final payment or pickup, not after the shipment reaches the port.

## Quick Answer

Before final payment or pickup, connect each finished product, SKU, model, manufacturing place and date, supporting test data, CPSC citations or testing exclusions, certificate point of contact, and broker handoff plan. Huang Sourcing can compare supplier files with visible order evidence in China, but the importer, broker, laboratory, compliance adviser, or counsel must decide filing, certification, and legal compliance.

## Post-Launch Status

- [CPSC's mandatory eFiling implementation announcement](https://www.cpsc.gov/Newsroom/News-Releases/2026/CPSC-Implements-Mandatory-eFiling-for-Certificates-of-Compliance-Targeting-Dangerous-Foreign-Imports) says eFiling is in effect for most imported regulated consumer products and changes how existing certificate data is transmitted rather than creating new testing or certification duties.
- The [CPSC eFiling FAQ](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/eFiling-Frequently-Asked-Questions-FAQ) says a finished-product certificate must identify the testing used to support certification and, from July 8, 2026, any testing exclusions relied on. It also says Disclaim PGA Message Sets are encouraged but not required.
- [FedEx's CPSC eFiling regulatory alert](https://www.fedex.com/en-ca/shipping-services/international/regulatory/mandatory-electronic-filing-requirements.html) tells customers importing covered products to provide certificate data with shipping documentation for transmission through the CPSC PGA message set in ACE.

## Buyer Release Checks

- Confirm whether the finished product is subject to a CPSC rule, ban, standard, or regulation that requires certification.
- Match each imported SKU, model, version, batch, and carton record to the product covered by the test report and certificate file.
- Collect Product ID, citation or testing exclusion codes, manufacture date and place, product test date, testing laboratory, and point-of-contact data.
- Decide whether the broker will receive a Full PGA Message Set data package or Product Registry certificate identifiers for a Reference PGA Message Set.
- Hold payment or pickup when product identity, test coverage, certificate data, or broker handoff remains unclear.

## Public Case Example

The [CPSC BUILT LUUM light-up tumbler recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Lifetime-Brands-Recalls-BUILT-LUUM-Light-Up-Tumblers-Due-to-Risk-of-Choking-and-Battery-Ingestion-Hazards) is a bounded product-identity example. CPSC reported that the LED compartment on the China-made product could separate and release button-cell batteries. The case does not show that eFiling would have prevented the issue and does not prove other products share the defect; it shows why certificate and test records should trace to the exact construction being shipped. Huang Sourcing was not involved in the product, testing, recall, or remedy.

## Scope Limits

Huang Sourcing can compare visible product, label, carton, packing, supplier, and document evidence against buyer-provided references in China. It does not file CPSC data, act as importer of record, select HTS codes, certify products, authenticate lab reports, provide legal advice, or guarantee customs acceptance, marketplace approval, compliance, or safety.
