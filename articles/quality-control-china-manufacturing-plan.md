# Quality Control in China Manufacturing: A Buyer's 5-Stage Plan

> Published: June 20, 2026
> Updated: October 31, 2026
> Original: https://www.huangsourcing.com/quality-control-china-manufacturing-plan

Use a five-stage China manufacturing QC plan and a buyer-to-factory control record to link specifications, checks, evidence, corrections, and shipment release.

Quality control in China manufacturing should begin before the factory starts bulk production, not when sealed cartons are waiting for pickup. A workable plan connects the approved supplier, controlled product requirement, reference sample, production checkpoint, inspection scope, and buyer release decision. Each stage should leave a named owner and an evidence handoff for the next one.

## Quick Answer

Use five connected stages: qualify the supplier and production site; freeze the specification and approved sample; confirm production readiness; inspect where defects can still be corrected; and make the payment or shipment decision against defined evidence. For each important requirement, keep one handoff row with its current revision, supplier acknowledgment, check owner, evidence, open exception, and buyer disposition.

## Buyer–factory–inspector handoff

For each requirement that could change an order decision, keep one shared control record:

1. **Requirement and revision:** Record the PO, SKU, requirement ID, approved drawing or file revision, reference sample, accepted exception, and supplier acknowledgment.
2. **Stage, method, and acceptance basis:** Name the check stage, method or specialist test, presented lot, sample basis, acceptance rule, and inspector scope.
3. **Result and evidence:** Keep the checked quantity, date, findings, identified photos or measurements, and any access or sampling limits.
4. **Exception and release:** Record the affected lot, containment and correction owner, evidence deadline, re-check access, and the buyer-designated release owner's written decision.

A buyer or qualified product owner must set the actual method, acceptance rule, and specialist review. This handoff is a Huang Sourcing editorial planning aid, not an ISO-required form or proof that every unit conforms. The website includes a clearly hypothetical packaging-artwork example; no client case or expert review is claimed.
