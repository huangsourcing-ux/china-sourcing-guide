# Crib Mattress Checks Before Shipping from China

> Published: September 30, 2026
> Original: https://www.huangsourcing.com/crib-mattress-checks-china

Before releasing China-made crib or play-yard mattresses, check exact host-model compatibility, qualified test evidence, labels, production lots, and cartons.

Before paying the balance or authorizing pickup for a China-made crib or aftermarket play-yard mattress, identify the exact mattress variant and every crib or play-yard model it claims to fit. Match that claim to qualified compatibility testing, the approved construction, labels, production lots, and cartons. Hold any variant whose host-model evidence or shipment identity is missing.

## Quick Answer

Use three gates before release: classify the exact mattress and intended host products; have the importer confirm the applicable U.S. rule set, test reports, and certificate scope; then compare sampled production, compatibility claims, labels, lots, and cartons with the approved version. A visual inspection can record mismatches but cannot prove infant-sleep safety or replace prescribed testing.

[CPSC crib-mattress guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Crib-Mattresses) distinguishes full-size crib mattresses, original-equipment non-full-size mattresses, and aftermarket mattresses for non-full-size cribs or play yards. For aftermarket products, a generic size description is insufficient: compatibility is tied to each intended host model.

## Release Checklist

1. **Freeze the product and host models.** List each mattress SKU and version, claimed compatible crib or play-yard model, original mattress reference, factory, lot, quantity, and carton range.
2. **Map qualified evidence.** Reconcile applicable rules, host-model compatibility testing, sample identity, construction, flammability evidence where applicable, laboratory scope, and Children’s Product Certificate with the responsible importer and qualified lab.
3. **Compare production and claims.** Sample named lots for dimensions, thickness, flatness, support structure, cover, seams, approved changes, label and instruction text, retail packaging, and carton identity.
4. **Hold or release named scope.** Segregate unmatched host claims, changed builds, missing reports, failed fit checks, wrong labels, and mixed cartons. Release only after documented qualified disposition and repeat checks.

## Public Case Example

The [CPSC Voomf recall of August 6, 2026](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Play-Yard-and-Crib-Mattresses-Recalled-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Entrapment-and-Fire-Hazards-Violate-Mandatory-Standards-for-Mattresses-Sold-on-Amazon-by-Voomf) covered about 2,401 China-made play-yard and crib mattresses. CPSC said the play-yard products might not adequately fit certain play yards, while the full-sized crib mattresses separately violated the mandatory mattress-set flammability standard. It reported one inadequate-fit report and no injuries. The notice identifies model BYL001 for the tri-fold play-yard mattress, but does not publish a complete laboratory file or establish a root cause for other suppliers.

A separate [July 2 CPSC Criblike warning](https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings/2026/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-Criblike-Mattresses-Immediately-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Entrapment-or-Suffocation-Violates-Mandatory-Standards-for-Mattresses-Sold-on-Amazon-by-Mengna) defined affected models and a narrow model/date and host-crib exception. These records support model-specific evidence and precise lot decisions; they do not prove another mattress is defective. Huang Sourcing did not participate in either case.

## Evidence and Limits

Hold a variant if a claimed host model lacks supporting testing, the production build differs from the tested sample, the fit check fails, a required warning or model label is missing, or cartons mix untraceable versions. The importer and qualified specialists decide classification, testing, certification, correction, and legal release. [CPSC third-party testing guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Testing-Certification/Third-Party-Testing) and [Children’s Product Certificate guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Testing-Certification/Childrens-Product-Certificate) describe those responsibilities. [Babylist’s current recall roundup](https://www.babylist.com/hello-baby/product-recalls-2026) also covers Voomf; it is an attention signal, not proof of search volume.
