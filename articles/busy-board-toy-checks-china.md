# Busy Board Toy Checks Before Shipping from China

> Published: September 26, 2026
> Original: https://www.huangsourcing.com/busy-board-toy-checks-china

Before releasing China-made busy boards, match the intended age, attached parts, painted hardware, accepted-lab evidence, CPC, model, lot, labels, and cartons.

Before paying the balance or authorizing pickup for a China-made busy board, identify the intended age, exact model, every attached part and coating, approved sample, qualified test evidence, production lots, retail packs, and cartons. Hold a model or lot when a detachable part, painted component, or changed construction is not covered by the approved evidence.

## Quick Answer

Use an age-and-evidence gate, a component-and-coating gate, and a model-and-lot release gate. A qualified party determines applicable U.S. rules and testing; buyer-side inspection compares sampled finished boards with the tested version and approved files. Release only named lots and cartons after any mismatch is resolved and documented.

## Before Shipment Checklist

1. Freeze the intended age, exact model, sales listing, approved sample, component list, factory, and production lots.
2. Match complete accepted-lab reports, tested-sample photos, applicable-rule list, Children's Product Certificate, coating records, and material-change history to the finished board.
3. Sample named lots and document knobs, zippers, latches, fasteners, edges, coatings, tracking marks, warnings, retail packs, and carton IDs.
4. Quarantine failures or undocumented variants. Record qualified disposition, any required retest, correction, repeat inspection, and the exact released cartons.

## Public Case and Decision

In [CPSC recall 26-775](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Esjay-US-Recalls-Toddler-Busy-Board-Montessori-Toys-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Choking-Violates-Small-Parts-Ban), announced September 17, 2026, red knobs on an Esjay toddler busy board could detach. CPSC described an under-three small-parts-ban violation, about 276,000 affected units, and no reported incidents or injuries. The buyer lesson is to match attachment construction and the tested sample to actual production lots; this recall does not establish that other busy boards are defective.

Separate [CPSC activity-board](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Melissa-and-Doug-Recalls-Fire-Truck-Activity-Board-Toys-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Choking-Hazard-Sold-Exclusively-at-Target) and [Lontcs busy-board](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/SQT-Recalls-Lontcs-Busy-Boards-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-from-Lead-Poisoning-Violate-Federal-Lead-Paint-Ban) notices document different detachable-part and lead-paint hazards. They show why a check of one feature cannot clear the others. Huang Sourcing did not participate in these recalls.

## Scope Limits

The U.S. importer and qualified specialists must determine intended age, applicable rules, test methods, and certification. [CPSC small-parts guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Business-Education/Business-Guidance/Small-Parts-for-Toys-and-Childrens-Products) explains that a part may be present or liberated during prescribed use-and-abuse testing. Visual sampling cannot replace those tests or guarantee every unit in a lot. Hold changed components, coatings, inconsistent age claims, missing evidence, and untraceable cartons until a documented qualified decision closes the gap.
