# Pressure Washer Checks Before Shipping from China

> Published: August 21, 2026
> Updated: September 28, 2026
> Original: https://www.huangsourcing.com/pressure-washer-checks-china

Check GFCI, cord, exact model, recall or warning status, electrical evidence, and cartons before releasing pressure washers from China.

Before paying the balance or allowing pickup for electric pressure washers made in China, freeze the destination, exact model, electrical rating, integral ground-fault circuit-interrupter configuration, power-cord length, plug and strain relief, pump and motor version, hose, spray gun, nozzles, labels, instructions, qualified evidence, production lot, and carton map. Hold the lot when those elements do not describe one controlled production version.

## Quick Answer

Use four linked gates: importer-led requirements mapping, qualified evidence for the exact electrical construction, buyer-side comparison of sampled production with approved references, and traceable correction or release. Routine inspection can document identity, the presence and configuration of an integral GFCI, cord and strain-relief construction, accessories, workmanship, markings, quantities, and pack-out. It cannot prove GFCI trip performance, dielectric strength, insulation integrity, ingress protection, pressure endurance, or legal compliance.

## September 2026 public case update

On September 24, the [U.S. CPSC warned consumers to stop using FOTING pressure washers](https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings/2026/CPSC-Warns-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-FOTING-Pressure-Washers-Immediately-Due-to-Serious-Shock-and-Electrocution-Hazards-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death). The agency reported missing integrated immersion protection, also called an integral GFCI, and an inadequate-length power cord. CPSC said Success Way Ltd. of China had not agreed to an acceptable recall. This is a safety warning, not a recall notice. CPSC reported one missing-GFCI report, six short-cord reports, and no injuries.

On September 17, the [CPSC separately recalled 329 LANCHEZ model KLC-BULL135C pressure washers](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/LANCHEZ-Pressure-Washers-Recalled-Due-to-Serious-Risk-of-Injury-or-Death-from-Shock-and-Electrocution-Hazards). The notice limits the recall to washers lacking a GFCI or adequate-length cord and reports one missing-GFCI observation with no injuries. These records describe different products and parties. They do not establish a shared factory or prove a defect in every China-made washer.

## Buyer release decision

1. Check current authority notices against the exact brand, model, electrical configuration, product label, component lot, batch, and carton map before final payment or pickup.
2. Quarantine potentially affected inventory while the importer confirms scope and follows official instructions. A factory retrofit, different plug, external GFCI outlet, new sticker, or supplier assurance does not independently clear a product identified in a public warning or recall.
3. For a different or revised model, obtain qualified assessment of the GFCI and cord construction and whether the existing reports cover the finished version. Compare sampled units with approved references and document the models, quantities, and cartons authorized for release.

Buyer-side inspection can document identity, visible construction, and traceability. It cannot prove GFCI trip behavior, dielectric strength, insulation integrity, water ingress performance, or legal compliance.
