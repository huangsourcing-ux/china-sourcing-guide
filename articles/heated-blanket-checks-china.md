# Heated Blanket Checks Before Shipping from China

> Published: September 24, 2026  
> Original: https://www.huangsourcing.com/heated-blanket-checks-china

Before paying the balance or allowing pickup for heated blankets or heating pads made in China, identify the exact model and production lot. Match finished goods to the approved controller, power cord, connector, heating element, textile, labels, and carton records. Hold stock when a critical part or its evidence cannot be tied to the actual units.

## Quick answer

Use three separate gates. First, the importer and qualified specialists define destination requirements and test evidence for the exact product. Second, the factory controls the approved construction and all changes. Third, buyer-side inspection compares accessible production units, labels, quantities, and cartons with those references. A visual or short powered check cannot establish electrical or thermal safety, long-term performance, or legal compliance.

## Release checklist

1. **Freeze the saleable version.** Map each retail SKU and exact model to factory, voltage, controller, cord, connector, element layout, textile, size, timer, labels, instructions, production lot, and cartons.
2. **Verify qualified evidence.** Map complete test or certification records to the same construction, sample, factory, model, and stated limitations.
3. **Compare production.** Use an approved sampling plan to check accessible parts, stitching, wire routing, connector fit, controls, markings, packaging, and agreed functions against signed references.
4. **Close the release scope.** Record failures, substitutions, affected quantities, corrective action, qualified disposition, repeat checks, and the exact lots and cartons allowed to move.

## Public case example

The [U.S. CPSC's September 17, 2026 JKMAX recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/JKMAX-Recalls-Heated-Blankets-and-Heating-Pads-Due-to-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards) covered about 195,000 heated blankets and heating pads imported from China. CPSC stated that their internal wire heating elements could overheat and reported 577 incidents of smoking, melting, or burning, including 53 burn injuries. The notice identifies model markings on the remote and packaging. [CBS News covered the recall](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/amazon-jkmax-heated-blanket-recall/) the following day.

A [separate CPSC OCTROT recall dated July 30, 2026](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Heated-Sherpa-Throws-and-Blankets-Recalled-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards-Sold-on-Amazon-by-OCTROT) described a cord that could detach and ignite. The two records concern different products and mechanisms. They show why buyers should control both the internal heating assembly and the cord or connector configuration; they do not prove another supplier or lot is defective. Huang Sourcing had no role in either case.

## What to request and when to hold

Request the PO, destination and responsible importer, exact models and quantities, approved sample, bill of materials, component and change records, complete qualified reports, labels and instructions, packing list, lot map, sample plan, and stop rules. Photograph model and lot identity on sampled products, detachable controllers, retail packs, and cartons. Keep inaccessible construction and untested performance clearly marked as open evidence.

Hold and escalate if model or critical component identity is missing, reports do not cover the build, the factory changed a controller, cord, connector, element, textile, or process without qualified disposition, a sampled unit fails, or cartons cannot be separated into cleared and unresolved lots. Release only the named versions, quantities, lots, and cartons after documented correction and any required qualified retesting.

Buyer-side sampling cannot certify electrical safety, prove thermal endurance, or determine every legal requirement for the destination. Those decisions belong to the importer and qualified technical parties.
