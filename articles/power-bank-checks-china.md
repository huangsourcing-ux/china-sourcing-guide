# Power Bank Quality Checks Before Shipping from China

> Published: July 27, 2026
> Updated: September 29, 2026
> Original: https://www.huangsourcing.com/power-bank-checks-china

Use recent CPSC power bank cases to check model, serial, lot, battery evidence, visible condition, and carton scope before payment or pickup in China.

Before releasing balance payment or pickup for power banks in China, screen the exact model against current recalls and warnings. Then verify that each production lot, serial system, approved construction, technical file, visible condition, retail pack, and carton describes the same product. Hold affected or unidentified stock, any unit with a heat or swelling signal, and batches whose changes or corrections cannot be traced.

## Quick Answer

Use three separate gates: qualified product-safety and laboratory review, buyer-side physical and document inspection, and dangerous-goods transport approval. A pre-shipment inspection can find visible and traceability problems, but it cannot prove lithium-cell safety, predict latent failure, issue a test report, or guarantee carrier or marketplace acceptance.

## September 2026 public case and release decision

The [CPSC's September 24 NEWDERY recall](https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/NEWDERY-Power-Banks-Recalled-Due-to-Fire-and-Burn-Hazards-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-Sold-on-Amazon) covers about 21,380 China-made ZHX-PB22 power banks. The notice reports nine explosions or ignitions, including one reported serious fire with $2 million in property damage and one reported burn injury. A separate [September 17 CPSC warning](https://www.cpsc.gov/Warnings/2026/CPSC-Urges-Consumers-to-Stop-Using-Hererod-Jump-Starters-Power-Banks-Immediately-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-and-Death-from-Fire-Hazard) names Hererod PW40 and EW40 jump-starter/power banks and 16 fire reports. A warning and a recall are different agency actions.

Before balance payment or pickup, compare the order's exact model and aliases with current official notices; map model, serial or date code, production lot, retail pack, and carton to the approved specification and technical files. Hold matching or unidentified stock, isolate abnormal units, and obtain a qualified disposition for changed battery construction or evidence gaps. Neither public case proves a fault in another supplier's goods, and an ordinary visual inspection cannot establish internal battery safety. Huang Sourcing was not involved in either case.
